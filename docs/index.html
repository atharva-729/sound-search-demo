      05 · Fine print

      What the numbers don't show

      • Labels are strict. A ✗ can still sound right: a giggle counts as laughter, but a crowd laughing inside an “applause” clip doesn't.
      • Neighbours confuse neighbours. “Clearing throat” and “shouting” reach only 4 of 10: their nearest clips are coughs and screams. Here one plain word beat a richer description (0 → 4 of 10).
      • The example chips are the best case. “Angry clip → similar” starts from the most typical angry clip; chart 02 shows the average.
      • Podcast clips are 20 s long, the others 2–10 s, so a short cough inside a podcast is diluted. Podcasts also cluster by show (same hosts, mic and music).
      • FSD50K audio plays once its zip is imported (it was embedded in Colab).
